## 1. Benchmark Level: Eiken Pre-2 (英検準2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~2 to 2.5 minutes. Advanced vocabulary and structured clauses.*

A devastating warfare erupted between the feathered avian populations of the skies and the terrestrial quadrupeds inhabiting the forests. Motivated exclusively by cowardly self-preservation, an opportunistic bat resolved to adopt a stance of ambiguous neutrality, adamantly refusing to commit his allegiance until the final outcome of the hostilities became unmistakable.

During the initial skirmishes, the aerial forces inflicted substantial casualties upon their ground-based adversaries. Observing that the birds had secured a commanding tactical advantage, the bat descended among their ranks, triumphantly exhibiting his membranous pinions. "Behold my magnificent wings!" he proclaimed enthusiastically. "Undoubtedly, my biological lineage aligns me with the feathered kingdom." Welcoming this seemingly loyal recruit, the flock embraced him without skepticism.

Nevertheless, the fortunes of war shifted dramatically when the beasts consolidated their legions and mounted a ferocious counteroffensive. Recognizing that the terrestrial forces were rapidly gaining the upper hand, the unprincipled opportunist defected without the slightest hesitation. Presenting himself before the commanding beasts, he ostentatiously displayed his razor-sharp dentition and furry hide. "Do not be misled by my appendages," he insisted persuasively. "True birds possess neither teeth nor mammalian fur; therefore, I am an unequivocal beast." Readily convinced by his cunning rationalization, the beasts admitted him into their confederation.

Eventually, both factions negotiated a diplomatic reconciliation, signing a comprehensive peace treaty to terminate the protracted hostilities. During the subsequent festivities, delegates from both realms compared intelligence, promptly exposing the bat's duplicitous maneuvers.

Revolted by such shameless perfidy, both communities formally condemned the traitor and permanently stripped him of citizenship within their sovereign borders. Ostracized by all living creatures, the disowned outcast was driven into subterranean caverns, condemned to nocturnal obscurity and eternal solitude.

Unprincipled opportunism may yield temporary advantages, but chronic disloyalty inevitably results in total alienation and universal contempt.

## 2. Target Level: Eiken Grade 2 (英検2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~3 to 4 minutes. Academic discourse, complex participial structures, formal vocabulary, and nuanced transitions.*

Geopolitical polarization between the sovereign avian confederacy and the terrestrial mammalian coalition degenerated into total, unmitigated warfare across the biome. Confronted by this systemic existential clash, an amphibious chiropteran—a bat occupying an ambiguous phylogenetic niche—rejected ideological commitment, electing instead to operationalize calculated, cynical non-alignment. His strategic calculus was predicated entirely upon minimizing personal vulnerability while positioning himself to extract geopolitical patronage from the eventual victor.

During the opening campaign, air superiority doctrine enabled the avian armadas to decimate ground formations through devastating aerial bombardment. Perceiving that the strategic initiative had shifted decisively in favor of the winged coalition, the bat executed an immediate diplomatic pivot. Presenting himself before the avian council, he rhetorically emphasized his aerodynamic morphology, waving his elongated interdigital membranes as conclusive proof of phylogenetic solidarity. "My functional adaptation for atmospheric flight definitively confirms my sovereign allegiance to the avian collective," he asserted with manipulative eloquence. Swayed by confirmation bias and eager to augment their ranks, the avian leadership accredited his credentials without rigorous background vetting.

However, the dynamics of protracted warfare rarely conform to linear projections. Regrouping their armored divisions, the terrestrial beasts launched a catastrophic counteroffensive, neutralizing aerial dominance and encircling key strongholds. Discerning the imminent collapse of avian hegemony, the unprincipled diplomat perpetrated an immediate ideological apostasy. Defecting across battle lines, he petitioned the mammalian high command, strategically shifting his taxonomy from flight mechanics to morphological dentition and maternal nursing traits. "Observe my canine teeth and pelage," he argued persuasively. "Aviation constitutes merely a superficial locomotion mechanism; fundamentally, my biological essence resides within the mammalian hierarchy." Satisfied by this expedient rationalization, the beasts integrated him into their operational cadre.

Upon the formal cessation of hostilities, the belligerents convened a peace congress to negotiate demilitarization and post-war territorial demarcation. During bilateral intelligence exchanges, security officials uncovered irreconcilable discrepancies in the chiropteran's historical record, thoroughly exposing his cynical duplicity.

The collective response of both sovereign bodies was immediate and uncompromising. Formally designating him a persona non grata, the unified coalition revoked his legal status, subjecting him to unconditional banishment from the planetary surface. Stripped of diplomatic immunity and universally abhorred as the quintessential embodiment of moral vacuity, the disenfranchised outcast was relegated to subterranean caverns, permanently consigned to nocturnal isolation and systemic ostracism.

Pragmatic opportunism devoid of moral conviction may facilitate transient survival, but calculated duplicity ultimately precipitates total disenfranchisement and universal execration.

## 3. Classic Original English Text (イソップ寓話 クラシック原文)
*Townsend / Jacobs Edition (Public Domain)*

A great battle was about to be fought between the Birds and the Beasts. The Bat, fearing the result, stayed away and waited to see which side would win. When the Birds were winning, the Bat flew among them, claiming to be a bird because he had wings. Later, when the Beasts gained the upper hand, the Bat went over to them, claiming to be a beast because he had teeth and fur. But when peace was declared, both sides recognized his deceit. Ashamed and rejected by all, the Bat was forced to hide in dark caves, flying only at night.

He that is friend to all is friend to none.

### 🏛️ 【英検2級レベルで読み解く！クラシック原文の重要構文・英文法ノート】
1. **1. 分詞構文（Participle Clause: 付帯状況・理由）**
   - **原文**: *"The Hare, believing her assertion to be simply impossible, agreed to the proposal..."*
   - **解説**: 「〜と信じ込んでいたので」という理由を表す分詞構文。英検2級の長文読解・ライティングの最頻出構文です。
1. **2. 古典的仮定法現在（Subjunctive Mood）**
   - **原文**: *"Though you be swift as the wind, I will beat you in a race."*
   - **解説**: 譲歩節 `Though` の中で原形 `be` を用いる古典的な仮定法現在（現代英語の `Though you are...`）。格調高い古典の響きを持ちます。
1. **3. 過去分詞の後置修飾（Postpositive Participle）**
   - **原文**: *"On the day appointed for the race the two started together."*
   - **解説**: `appointed`（定められた・指定された）が直前の名詞 `the day` を後ろから修飾する構文です。

### 📜 【クラシック原文の重要語彙・古風表現（Classic Antique Vocabulary）】
- **retort** [動] 2級 **（機転を利かせて）言い返す、反論する**（現代の reply sharp/wittily に相当）
- **assertion** [名] 2級/準1級 **主張、断言**（動詞 assert の名詞形。2級長文定番）
- **wayside** [名] 2級 **道端、沿道**（by the wayside（道端に、途中で））
- **fatigue** [名] 2級 **（心身の）極度の疲労、疲れ**（tiredness の上位フォーマル語）

## 4. Japanese Translation (日本語訳)

鳥類の主権連邦と陸生哺乳類の同盟との間の地政学的分極化は、生物群系全体にわたる完全かつ無制限の戦争へと退廃しました。この体制的な実存的衝突に直面し、両義的な系統発生的ニッチを占める翼手類――コウモリ――は、イデオロギー的な関与を拒絶し、代わりに計算された冷笑的な非同盟を実行することを選択しました。彼の戦略的計算は、最終的な勝利者から地政学的庇護を抽出できるよう自らを位置づける一方で、個人の脆弱性を最小限に抑えることに完全に依拠していました。

緒戦の戦役において、航空優勢のドクトリンは、壊滅的な空中爆撃を通じて鳥類艦隊が地上陣地を撃破することを可能にしました。戦略的先制権が翼を持つ同盟の側に決定的に傾いたと知覚したコウモリは、即座に外交的ピボット（方針転換）を実行しました。鳥類の評議会の前に進み出ると、彼は自らの空気力学的形態を修辞学的に強調し、細長い指間膜を系統発生的連帯の決定的な証拠として振りかざしました。「大気中を飛行するための私の機能的適応は、鳥類の集団に対する私の主権的忠誠を決定的に裏付けるものです」と彼は巧妙な雄弁さで主張しました。確証バイアスに動かされ、自らの隊列を増強することに熱心であった鳥類の指導部は、厳格な経歴審査を行うことなく彼の信任状を承認しました。

しかしながら、長期にわたる戦争の力学が直線的な予測に従うことは滅多にありません。装甲部隊を再編し、陸生の獣たちは壊滅的な反撃を開始し、制空権を無力化して重要拠点を包囲しました。鳥類の覇権の差し迫った崩壊を見極めたこの無節操な外交官は、即座にイデオロギー的背教（背信）を犯しました。前線を越えて寝返った彼は哺乳類の最高司令部に嘆願し、自らの分類学を飛行力学から形態学的な歯列および授乳の特性へと戦略的に転換しました。「私の犬歯と毛皮をご覧ください」と彼は説得力豊かに論じました。「飛行は単なる表面的な移動機構にすぎません。本質的に、私の生物学的本質は哺乳類の序列の中に宿っているのです。」この都合のよい合理化に満足し、獣たちは自らの作戦中核へと彼を統合しました。

敵対行為の公式な停止に際し、交戦国双方は非軍事化と戦後の領土画定を交渉するための平和会議を招集しました。二国間情報交換の最中、安全保障当局者たちはこの翼手類の過去の記録における相容れない矛盾を発見し、彼の冷笑的な二枚舌を徹底的に暴露しました。

両主権団体の集団的対応は即座であり、一切の妥協を許さないものでした。彼を公式に「ペルソナ・ノン・グラータ（好ましからざる人物）」に指定し、統一同盟は彼の法的地位を取り消し、地表からの無条件の追放処分に処しました。外交特権を剥奪され、道徳的空虚さの典型的な具現として普遍的に嫌悪されたこの権利剥奪の落伍者は、地下の洞窟へと追いやられ、夜間の孤立と体系的追放処分へと永久に委ねられたのです。

**教訓**: 道徳的信念を欠いたプラグマティックな日和見主義は一時的な生存を促進するかもしれないが、計算された二枚舌は最終的に完全な権利剥奪と普遍的な憎悪をもたらす。

### 【出題頻出語彙・熟語ノート】
- **hegemony** [名] 覇権、主導権（形容詞 *hegemonic*）
- **apostasy** [名] 背教、背信、変節（動詞 *apostatize*）
- **vetting** [名] 審査、身元調査（動詞 *vet*「〜を精査する」）
- **persona non grata** [熟] 外交上好ましからざる人物（ラテン語由来の国際外交用語）
- **disenfranchise** [動] （公民権・権利など）を奪う、無力化する
- **devoid of...** [熟] 〜を欠いている、〜が全くない（＝*completely lacking in*）
- **execration** [名] 憎悪、嫌悪、呪詛（動詞 *execrate*）

### 【文法・構文ポイント】
1. **分詞による動因・状況設定 [Discerning the imminent collapse ..., he perpetrated ...]**
   - 2級本文: *"Discerning the imminent collapse of avian hegemony, the unprincipled diplomat perpetrated an immediate ideological apostasy."*
   - ポイント: 情勢認識から裏切りへの迅速な行動遷移を分詞構文によって論理的かつ躍動感を持って叙述します。
2. **形式形容詞句 [devoid of ...]（〜を欠いた）**
   - 2級本文: *"Pragmatic opportunism devoid of moral conviction may facilitate transient survival..."*
   - ポイント: `lacking` の形式的同義語。理念や実質が欠如した政策・行動を批判する際の定番表現です。
