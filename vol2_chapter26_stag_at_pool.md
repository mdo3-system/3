## 1. Benchmark Level: Eiken Grade 3 (英検3級レベル本文)
*Read in ~1.5 to 2 minutes. Baseline vocabulary and basic syntax.*

A graceful stag stepped toward the edge of a crystal-clear spring to quench his thirst. While gazing into the tranquil surface, he noticed his own reflection for the first time.

He admired his magnificent antlers, which spread out majestically like the branches of an oak tree. Filled with excessive vanity, he praised their extraordinary elegance. However, when he shifted his gaze toward his slender legs, he felt deeply disappointed. "How wretched and frail my legs appear!" he grumbled bitterly. "They are far too thin to match my splendid crown."

At that very instant, the distant baying of hounds echoed through the valley. A skilled hunter had spotted him and immediately launched a pursuit.

Instinctively, the stag bounded across the open plain, leaving his pursuers far behind thanks to the remarkable speed of his agile legs. Yet, as he plunged into a thick forest to seek shelter, his wide antlers became firmly entangled in the tangled boughs of the trees.

Struggling desperately in vain as the hounds rapidly closed in, the stag lamented, "Alas! How foolish I was to despise the humble legs that could have preserved my life, while taking excessive pride in the ornamental horns that have caused my destruction!"

We frequently undervalue what is truly beneficial and praise what ultimately brings about our downfall.

## 2. Target Level: Eiken Pre-2 (英検準2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~2.5 to 3.5 minutes. Advanced vocabulary, cause-and-effect transitions, participial constructions, and formal discourse.*

Approaching an undisturbed woodland reservoir to satisfy his dehydration, an aristocratic stag paused to contemplate his aquatic mirror image. Mesmerized by his own silhouette, the herbivore indulged in an intensive self-assessment dominated by superficial aesthetic criteria.

He marveled with profound gratification at his sprawling antlers, which ascended toward the sky in an elaborate architecture of calcified tines reminiscent of an imperial diadem. Nevertheless, this narcissistic reverie deteriorated into acute revulsion when his appraisal shifted downward toward his lower extremities. Observing his elongated, spindly limbs, he registered profound indignation: "What an appalling biological contradiction! How humiliating that such an awe-inspiring sovereign should be supported by limbs so wretchedly slender, devoid of muscular grandeur, and aesthetically offensive!"

Before he could indulge further in self-pity, the thunderous baying of hunting hounds shattered the forest equilibrium. Alerted to the imminent peril, the stag reacted instantaneously. Propelled by the remarkable elasticity and mechanical efficiency of his despised limbs, he accelerated across the open savanna with breathtaking agility, effortlessly outdistancing the equestrian hunters and their canine vanguard.

Tragically, seeking refuge within a dense arboreal sanctuary, the fleet-footed quadruped encountered catastrophic misfortune. His expansive, branching antlers became hopelessly snared within the dense canopy of low-hanging branches. Deprived of mobility by the very ornaments he idolized, his frantic lunges served merely to anchor him more securely to the timber.

As the baying pack converged to tear him apart, the doomed animal articulated a bitter realization: "What catastrophic delusion blinded my judgment! I despised the utilitarian limbs that granted me salvation, while venerating the decorative appendages that orchestrated my inescapable doom."

Humanity habitually denigrates functional utility in favor of ostentatious display, oblivious to the fact that ornamental vanity frequently precipitates ultimate ruin.

## 3. Classic Original English Text (イソップ寓話 クラシック原文)
*Townsend / Jacobs Edition (Public Domain)*

A Stag quenched his thirst at a clear lake, and gazed upon his reflection in the water. He greatly admired the size and variety of his horns, but felt much displeased with the spindly weakness of his legs. 

While he was still contemplating his form, a Lion sprang upon him. The Stag fled over the plain and outran him, for the strength of stags lies in their legs, but of the lion in his heart. The Stag kept ahead until he reached a dense wood, where his antlers became entangled in the overhanging branches. He could not free himself, and the Lion quickly overtook him. 

When on the point of death, the Stag cried: "Woe is me! What an ungrateful wretch I am! Those feet which I despised would have saved me, while the horns on which I prided myself have brought about my ruin."

We often despise what is most useful, and prize that which brings our doom.

### 🏛️ 【英検2級レベルで読み解く！クラシック原文の重要構文・英文法ノート】
1. **1. 分詞構文（Participle Clause: 付帯状況・理由）**
   - **原文**: *"The Hare, believing her assertion to be simply impossible, agreed to the proposal..."*
   - **解説**: 「〜と信じ込んでいたので」という理由を表す分詞構文。英検2級の長文読解・ライティングの最頻出構文です。
1. **2. 古典的仮定法現在（Subjunctive Mood）**
   - **原文**: *"Though you be swift as the wind, I will beat you in a race."*
   - **解説**: 譲歩節 `Though` の中で原形 `be` を用いる古典的な仮定法現在（現代英語の `Though you are...`）。格調高い古典の響きを持ちます。
1. **3. 過去分詞の後置修飾（Postpositive Participle）**
   - **原文**: *"On the day appointed for the race the two started together."*
   - **解説**: `appointed`（定められた・指定された）が直前の名詞 `the day` を後ろから修飾する構文です。

### 📜 【クラシック原文の重要語彙・古風表現（Classic Antique Vocabulary）】
- **retort** [動] 2級 **（機転を利かせて）言い返す、反論する**（現代の reply sharp/wittily に相当）
- **assertion** [名] 2級/準1級 **主張、断言**（動詞 assert の名詞形。2級長文定番）
- **wayside** [名] 2級 **道端、沿道**（by the wayside（道端に、途中で））
- **fatigue** [名] 2級 **（心身の）極度の疲労、疲れ**（tiredness の上位フォーマル語）

## 4. Japanese Translation (日本語訳)

脱水状態を癒すために人里離れた森林の貯水池へと近づいた高貴なオスの鹿は、水面に映る自らの鏡像をじっくり熟視するために立ち止まりました。自らのシルエットに心を奪われたその草食動物は、浅薄な審美的基準に支配された徹底的な自己評価に浸りました。

彼は、帝国の王冠を思わせる石灰化した枝角の精巧な構造をなして天空へと伸びる自らの広大な角を、深い満足感をもって驚嘆の目で見つめました。それにもかかわらず、その自己陶酔的な空想は、彼の評価が下方にある自らの下肢へと移ったとき、急激な嫌悪感へと堕落しました。自らの細長く頼りない足筋を観察した彼は、深い憤りを表明しました。「なんと恐るべき生物学的な矛盾だろう！これほど畏怖すべき主権者が、これほどみすぼらしく細く、筋肉の雄大さに欠け、美的に不快な足によって支えられているとは、なんという屈辱だろう！」

彼がさらなる自己憐憫に浸る間もなく、猟犬たちの雷のような遠吠えが森林の静寂と均衡を打ち砕きました。差し迫った危機を警告された鹿は、瞬時に反応しました。彼自身が蔑んでいた足の並外れた弾力性と機械的効率性に推進され、彼は息をのむような俊敏さをもって開放的なサバンナを加速し、騎馬の猟師たちとその先鋒たる猟犬の群れを難なく引き離しました。

悲劇的なことに、鬱蒼とした樹木が生い茂る保護区の中へ避難所を求めたとき、この俊足の四足動物は壊滅的な不幸に遭遇しました。彼の広大に枝分かれした枝角が、低く垂れ下がった枝の濃密な樹冠の中に絶望的なほど引っかかってしまったのです。自らが崇拝していた装飾そのものによって機動力を奪われ、彼の狂乱的な身悶えは、ただ彼を木々にさらに固く固定するだけに役立ちました。

遠吠えをあげる群れが彼を引き裂こうと押し寄せる中、破滅に瀕した動物は苦い悟りを口にしました。「なんと壊滅的な妄想が私の判断力を狂わせていたことか！私に救済を与えてくれた実用的な足を蔑む一方で、私の逃れられない破滅を画策した装飾的な付属器官を崇め奉っていたとは！」

**教訓**: 人類は派手な見せびらかしを好んで実用的な有用性を軽視しがちであるが、装飾的な虚栄心がしばしば決定的な破滅を引き起こすという事実に気づいていない。

### 【出題傾向・文法解説ノート】

### 【出題頻出語彙・熟語ノート】
- **contemplate** [動] 〜を熟考する、じっと見つめる（名詞 *contemplation*）
- **deteriorate** [動] 悪化する、低下する（*worsen* のフォーマル語）
- **revulsion** [名] 嫌悪、激変、激しい反感
- **appraisal** [名] 評価、鑑定、査定（動詞 *appraise*）
- **propel** [動] 〜を推進する、駆り立てる（名詞 *propulsion*）
- **deprive A of B** [構] AからBを奪う（受動態 *be deprived of...*）
- **utilitarian** [形] 実用的な、功利主義の（名詞 *utility*）
- **venerate** [動] 〜を崇敬する、敬う（*respect deeply*）
- **in favor of** [熟] 〜を支持して、〜を優先して
- **precipitate** [動] （悪い事態など）を引き起こす、早める

### 【文法・構文ポイント】
1. **剥奪を表す構文 [deprive A of B] の分詞構文**
   - 準2級本文: *"Deprived of mobility by the very ornaments he idolized..."*
   - ポイント: `deprive A of B`（AからBを奪う）が受動態の分詞構文となった形。「機動力を奪われて…」と原因・状況を客観的に示す準2級・2級の重要構文です。
2. **前置詞句 [in favor of ...]（〜を優先して、〜を選んで）**
   - 準2級本文: *"Humanity habitually denigrates functional utility in favor of ostentatious display..."*
   - ポイント: 「Aを退けてBを選ぶ」という対比の文脈で用いられる最重要イディオム。長文読解の選択肢でも対比関係を見抜くキーワードとなります。
