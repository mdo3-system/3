## 1. Benchmark Level: Eiken Pre-2 (英検準2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~2 to 2.5 minutes. Advanced vocabulary and structured clauses.*

A modest agrarian couple lived in humble circumstances until fortune smiled upon them through an extraordinary goose. Remarkably, this singular bird produced a solid gold egg at dawn every single day. By regularly converting these precious items into currency, the peasants rapidly elevated their standard of living and accumulated substantial savings without enduring excessive labor.

Notwithstanding this miraculous source of consistent prosperity, the farmer became increasingly dissatisfied with the daily pace of enrichment. Consumed by an insatiable lust for rapid wealth, he began to speculate about the bird's internal anatomy. "Surely," he reasoned impatiently to his spouse, "the creature's interior must contain a massive reservoir of molten gold. If we extract the entirety of the treasure simultaneously, we will instantly achieve unimaginable prosperity."

Persuaded by this seductive fallacy, the couple resolved to slaughter the goose in order to seize the concealed riches. Without pausing to reflect on the potential consequences, they terminated the creature's life and dissected its body.

Much to their utter horror and mortification, however, they discovered that the goose's anatomy differed in no respect from that of any common waterfowl. Not a single particle of precious metal lay hidden within.

Confronted with the irreversible tragedy of their own making, the couple succumbed to bitter remorse. In their rash attempt to obtain instantaneous abundance, they had extinguished the very source of their daily sustenance.

Unchecked avarice blinds judgment and inevitably destroys the sustainable foundations of genuine well-being.

## 2. Target Level: Eiken Grade 2 (英検2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~3 to 4 minutes. Academic discourse, complex participial structures, formal vocabulary, and nuanced transitions.*

In an impoverished rural enclave, an agrarian household eked out a precarious existence until an unprecedented stroke of biological anomaly altered their financial trajectory. One of their domestic fowl, an outwardly unexceptional goose, began depositing an egg of immaculate, solid gold at each successive dawn. By methodically liquidating these daily windfalls at municipal markets, the proprietors systematically dismantled their generational poverty, transitioning from marginal subsistence to an enviable degree of socio-economic security.

However, the psychological dynamics of sudden wealth rarely promote enduring temperance. Rather than remaining content with a reliable, compounding income stream, the patriarch grew increasingly intolerant of chronological constraints. Corrupted by cognitive distortions that conflated biological productivity with infinite inorganic reserves, he formulated a catastrophic financial conjecture. He postulated that the waterfowl's anatomical core must house an immense subterranean matrix of consolidated bullion. Proceeding from this delusional premise, he rationalized that preemptively liquidating the entire capital reserve through immediate surgical intervention would compress decades of wealth accumulation into a single instant.

Swayed by this disastrous economic calculus and prioritizing immediate gratification over sustainable returns, the couple executed the terminal vivisection of their singular asset. Stripping away the creature's organic layers with feverish anticipation, they subjected the visceral cavity to meticulous scrutiny.

To their paralyzed stupefaction and profound disillusionment, empirical reality asserted itself with brutal finality: the bird's internal physiological composition was entirely indistinguishable from that of any mundane avian specimen. Not the slightest trace of precious auriferous substance existed within the biological carcass.

The realization of their catastrophic miscalculation precipitated profound despair. Through a reckless obsession with maximizing immediate liquidity, they had irrevocably obliterated the self-renewing capital engine that had underwritten their prosperity.

Unregulated cupidity and shortsighted impatience inevitably subvert sustainable prosperity, demonstrating that the destruction of generative capital in pursuit of immediate gratification yields total insolvency.

## 3. Classic Original English Text (イソップ寓話 クラシック原文)
*Townsend / Jacobs Edition (Public Domain)*

A Cottager and his wife had a Hen which laid a golden egg every day. They supposed that the Hen must contain a great lump of gold in its inside, and in order to get the gold, they killed it. Having done so, they found to their surprise that the Hen differed in no respect from their other hens. The foolish pair, hoping to become rich all at once, thus deprived themselves of the gain of which they were assured day by day.

Thinking to get at once all the gold, they lost even the little they had.

### 🏛️ 【英検2級レベルで読み解く！クラシック原文の重要構文・英文法ノート】
1. **1. 分詞構文（Participle Clause: 付帯状況・理由）**
   - **原文**: *"The Hare, believing her assertion to be simply impossible, agreed to the proposal..."*
   - **解説**: 「〜と信じ込んでいたので」という理由を表す分詞構文。英検2級の長文読解・ライティングの最頻出構文です。
1. **2. 古典的仮定法現在（Subjunctive Mood）**
   - **原文**: *"Though you be swift as the wind, I will beat you in a race."*
   - **解説**: 譲歩節 `Though` の中で原形 `be` を用いる古典的な仮定法現在（現代英語の `Though you are...`）。格調高い古典の響きを持ちます。
1. **3. 過去分詞の後置修飾（Postpositive Participle）**
   - **原文**: *"On the day appointed for the race the two started together."*
   - **解説**: `appointed`（定められた・指定された）が直前の名詞 `the day` を後ろから修飾する構文です。

### 📜 【クラシック原文の重要語彙・古風表現（Classic Antique Vocabulary）】
- **retort** [動] 2級 **（機転を利かせて）言い返す、反論する**（現代の reply sharp/wittily に相当）
- **assertion** [名] 2級/準1級 **主張、断言**（動詞 assert の名詞形。2級長文定番）
- **wayside** [名] 2級 **道端、沿道**（by the wayside（道端に、途中で））
- **fatigue** [名] 2級 **（心身の）極度の疲労、疲れ**（tiredness の上位フォーマル語）

## 4. Japanese Translation (日本語訳)

貧困にあえぐ農村の一角で、ある小作農家が不安定な生活を辛うじて営んでいましたが、生物学的な異変という前例のない幸運の巡り合わせが彼らの財務の軌道を一変させました。彼らが飼育していた家禽の1羽、外見上はごく普通のガチョウが、夜明けが訪れるたびに純粋な純金の卵を産み落とし始めたのです。地方市場でこれらの毎日の棚ぼたの産物を計画的に現金化することにより、所有者たちは何世代にもわたる貧困を組織的に解消し、限界的な最低限の生活から誰もが羨む社会経済的安定へと移行しました。

しかしながら、突発的な富の心理力学が永続的な自制心を促すことは極めて稀です。信頼性の高い複利的な収入の流れに満足し続けるどころか、家長は時間の制約に対して次第に不寛容になっていきました。生物学的生産性と無機物の無限の埋蔵量とを混同する認知的歪みに蝕まれ、彼は破滅的な財務的推測を組み立てました。この水鳥の解剖学的中心核には、凝縮された金塊の莫大な地下母岩が収蔵されているに違いないと彼は仮定したのです。この妄想的な前提から出発し、即座の外科的介入を通じて資本準備の全体を先手を打って清算すれば、何十年もの富の蓄積をわずか一瞬に圧縮できると彼は合理化しました。

この破滅的な経済計算に動かされ、持続可能な収益よりも即時的な満足を優先した夫婦は、自らの唯一無二の資産に対して致命的な生体解剖を実行しました。熱に浮かされた期待をもってその生き物の肉体組織を剥ぎ取り、彼らは内臓の腔所を細心の注意を払って綿密に精査しました。

彼らが麻痺するほどの呆然と深甚なる幻滅を覚えたことには、経験的現実が残酷な決定性をもって主張されました。鳥の体内における生理学的組成は、いかなる日常的な鳥類標本とも完全に区別がつかなかったのです。生体解剖された死骸の内側には、極小の金を含有する物質の痕跡すら存在しませんでした。

自らの破滅的な誤算を認識したことは、深甚なる絶望を引き起こしました。即時的な流動性の最大化に対する無謀な執着を通じて、彼らは自らの繁栄を支えていた自己更新型の資本エンジンを不可逆的に消滅させてしまったのです。

**教訓**: 制御されない強欲と近視眼的な焦燥は、持続可能な繁栄を必然的に覆す。即時的な満足を追求して生産的資本を破壊することは、完全な破産をもたらす。

### 【出題頻出語彙・熟語ノート】
- **windfall** [名] 棚ぼたの利益、思いがけない遺産・臨時収入
- **postulate** [動] （〜を前提として）仮定する、要求する
- **gratification** [名] 満足、充足（*delayed/immediate gratification*）
- **irrevocably** [副] 取り返しのつかないほどに、決定的に（形容詞 *irrevocable*）
- **obliterate** [動] （痕跡・存在など）を完全に消滅させる、破壊する
- **underwrite** [動] （費用など）を負担・保証する、引き受ける
- **insolvency** [名] 破産、債務超過（形容詞 *insolvent*）

### 【文法・構文ポイント】
1. **比較と優先順位を示す動名詞構文 [prioritizing A over B]**
   - 2級本文: *"prioritizing immediate gratification over sustainable returns, the couple executed..."*
   - ポイント: 「持続可能なリターンよりも短期的な快楽を優先する」という選択の対比。環境・金融・個人の行動選択エッセイで汎用性の極めて高い表現です。
2. **無生物主語構文 [The realization ... precipitated profound despair]**
   - 2級本文: *"The realization of their catastrophic miscalculation precipitated profound despair."*
   - ポイント: 「誤算に気づいて絶望した」という心理変化を、認識（主語）が絶望を加速・誘発した（動詞＋目的語）として格調高く提示します。
