## 1. Benchmark Level: Eiken Grade 4 (英検4級レベル本文)
*Read in ~1 to 1.5 minutes. Baseline vocabulary and basic syntax.*

Once, a poor slave named Androcles ran away from his cruel master. He hid in a dark cave in the forest.

Soon, a big lion entered the cave. Androcles was terrified, but the lion did not attack him. The lion was crying in pain because a sharp thorn was stuck in his paw. 

Androcles approached the lion bravely and pulled out the thorn. The lion felt relieved and licked the man's hand like a dog. They lived together in the cave as friends.

A few months later, soldiers found Androcles and took him back to the city. The king decided to feed him to a hungry lion in a large stadium.

Many people came to watch. When the cage opened, a ferocious lion rushed toward Androcles. But when the lion reached him, it stopped. It recognized its old friend and began to rub its head against his legs affectionately.

The king was amazed by this wonderful sight. After he heard their story, the king freed both Androcles and the faithful lion.

Kindness is never forgotten.

## 2. Target Level: Eiken Grade 3 (英検3級レベル本文)
*Read in ~2 to 3 minutes. Upgraded vocabulary, sophisticated sentence structures, and idioms.*

A slave named Androcles once escaped from a harsh master who treated him poorly. While wandering through a vast forest, he discovered a quiet cave where he could hide safely.

Suddenly, a huge lion appeared at the entrance, groaning in agony. Although Androcles was trembling with fear, he noticed that the beast was limping. The lion held up its swollen paw, which had a large, painful thorn deeply embedded in it. Overcoming his fear, Androcles carefully extracted the thorn and cleaned the wound. Deeply grateful, the gentle lion purred and became his loyal companion.

However, soldiers eventually captured Androcles and condemned him to be thrown to the wild beasts in the royal arena. On the day of punishment, an enormous crowd gathered to witness the execution. A ferocious lion, which had been starved for days, was released into the ring. It roared fiercely and charged straight at Androcles.

Yet, as soon as the beast recognized the young man, it immediately knelt down and purred softly, licking his hands with affection. The emperor, who was astonished by this miraculous scene, called Androcles to explain. Moved by their touching bond, the emperor pardoned Androcles and granted total freedom to both the man and his loyal beast.

True gratitude knows no boundaries, and mercy is always rewarded.

## 3. Classic Original English Text (イソップ寓話 クラシック原文)
*Townsend / Jacobs Edition (Public Domain)*

A slave named Androcles once escaped from his master and fled into the forest. As he was wandering about there, he came upon a Lion lying down, groaning and moaning. At first he turned to flee, but finding that the Lion did not pursue him, he turned back and went up to him. As he came near, the Lion put out his paw, which was all swollen and bleeding, and Androcles found that a huge thorn had got into it, and was causing all the pain. He pulled out the thorn and bound up the paw of the Lion, who was soon able to rise and lick the hand of Androcles like a dog. Then the Lion took Androcles to his cave, and every day used to bring him meat from which the two lived. 

But shortly afterwards both Androcles and the Lion were captured, and the slave was sentenced to be thrown to the Lion, after the latter had been kept without food for several days. The Emperor and all his Court came to look on at the spectacle, and Androcles was led out into the middle of the arena. Soon the Lion was let loose from his den, and rushed bounding and roaring towards his victim. But as soon as he came near to Androcles he recognised his friend, and fawned upon him, and licked his hands like a friendly dog. The Emperor was astonished at this strange spectacle and summoned Androcles to him. Androcles told the Emperor all that had happened, and was at once pardoned and set free, while the Lion was also loosed into his native forest.

Gratitude is the sign of noble souls.

### 🏛️ 【英検2級レベルで読み解く！クラシック原文の重要構文・英文法ノート】
1. **1. 分詞構文（Participle Clause: 付帯状況・理由）**
   - **原文**: *"The Hare, believing her assertion to be simply impossible, agreed to the proposal..."*
   - **解説**: 「〜と信じ込んでいたので」という理由を表す分詞構文。英検2級の長文読解・ライティングの最頻出構文です。
1. **2. 古典的仮定法現在（Subjunctive Mood）**
   - **原文**: *"Though you be swift as the wind, I will beat you in a race."*
   - **解説**: 譲歩節 `Though` の中で原形 `be` を用いる古典的な仮定法現在（現代英語の `Though you are...`）。格調高い古典の響きを持ちます。
1. **3. 過去分詞の後置修飾（Postpositive Participle）**
   - **原文**: *"On the day appointed for the race the two started together."*
   - **解説**: `appointed`（定められた・指定された）が直前の名詞 `the day` を後ろから修飾する構文です。

### 📜 【クラシック原文の重要語彙・古風表現（Classic Antique Vocabulary）】
- **retort** [動] 2級 **（機転を利かせて）言い返す、反論する**（現代の reply sharp/wittily に相当）
- **assertion** [名] 2級/準1級 **主張、断言**（動詞 assert の名詞形。2級長文定番）
- **wayside** [名] 2級 **道端、沿道**（by the wayside（道端に、途中で））
- **fatigue** [名] 2級 **（心身の）極度の疲労、疲れ**（tiredness の上位フォーマル語）

## 4. Japanese Translation (日本語訳)

アンドロクロスという名の奴隷が、かつて自分を不当に扱っていた過酷な主人から逃亡しました。広大な森をさまよっている間に、彼は安全に身を隠すことができる静かな洞窟を見つけました。

突然、一頭の巨大なライオンが苦悶の声を上げながら入口に現れました。アンドロクロスは恐怖に震えましたが、その獣が足を引きずっていることに気づきました。ライオンは腫れ上がった前足を差し出しましたが、そこには大きく痛むトゲが深く突き刺さっていました。恐怖を克服したアンドロクロスは、慎重にトゲを抜き取り、傷口をきれいに手当てしました。深く感謝した心優しいライオンは喉をゴロゴロと鳴らし、彼の忠実な仲間となりました。

しかし、やがて兵士たちがアンドロクロスを捕らえ、彼は王室の円形闘技場で獰猛な野獣の餌食にされる判決を受けました。処刑の日、その光景を見届けようと膨大な群衆が集まりました。何日も飢えさせられていた凶暴なライオンが競技場へと解き放たれました。ライオンは激しく咆哮し、アンドロクロスを目がけてまっすぐに突進してきました。

しかし、その獣はその青年を見分けるやいなや、即座に身をかがめ、愛情を込めて彼の手を舐めながら優しく喉を鳴らしました。この奇跡的な光景に驚嘆した皇帝は、事情を説明させるためにアンドロクロスを呼び出しました。彼らの感動的な絆に心を打たれた皇帝はアンドロクロスを赦免し、その若者と忠実な獣の両方に完全な自由を与えました。

**教訓**: 真の感謝の心に境界はなく、他者への慈悲は必ず報われる。

### 【出題頻出語彙・熟語ノート】
- **escape from** [熟] 〜から逃亡する、脱出する（*escape from danger*）
- **wander** [動] さまよう、歩き回る（*wander through the woods*）
- **groan** [動] うめく、うめき声をあげる
- **limp** [動] 足を引きずって歩く
- **extract** [動] 〜を抜き取る、引き抜く（*pull out* のフォーマルな表現）
- **wound** [名] 傷、負傷（動詞としても頻出）
- **condemn** [動] 〜に刑を宣告する、非難する（*condemn A to B*）
- **witness** [動] 〜を目撃する、立ち会う（名詞：目撃者）
- **astonish** [動] 〜を大いに驚かせる（*be astonished by/at...*「〜に驚嘆する」）
- **gratitude** [名] 感謝の気持ち（形容詞 *grateful*）

### 【文法・構文ポイント】
1. **関係代名詞の目的格・主格修飾 [who / which]**
   - 3級本文: *"A slave named Androcles once escaped from a harsh master who treated him poorly."*
   - 3級本文: *"A ferocious lion, which had been starved for days, was released into the ring."*
   - ポイント: 先行詞が人なら `who`、動物や物なら `which` を用いて、後ろから詳しく説明を加える英検3級の最重要文法。
2. **[as soon as ...]（〜するやいなや）と受動態の完了 [had been + 過去分詞]**
   - 3級本文: *"Yet, as soon as the beast recognized the young man, it immediately knelt down..."*
   - ポイント: 「Aが〜するとすぐにBした」という時間的連続性を表す重要表現。過去の基準時点より前に起きた「何日も飢えさせられていた（had been starved）」という受動過去完了と合わせて、物語の臨場感を高めます。
