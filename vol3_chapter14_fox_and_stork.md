## 1. Benchmark Level: Eiken Pre-2 (英検準2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~2 to 2.5 minutes. Advanced vocabulary and structured clauses.*

A cunning fox and a dignified stork maintained an outward display of cordial friendship, although the fox harbored a petty desire to humiliate his avian companion. Under the pretense of hospitable generosity, the fox extended an invitation to the stork for an evening banquet at his den.

Upon her arrival, the host presented a fragrant broth served exclusively in remarkably flat, shallow earthenware dishes. While the fox's broad tongue enabled him to consume the liquid with consummate ease, the stork's elongated beak proved entirely unsuited for such wide tableware. Despite repeated attempts, she could merely dip the tip of her bill into the broth, remaining completely famished. Amused by her obvious distress, the fox feigned polite concern, insincerely lamenting her apparent lack of appetite.

Masking her resentment behind impeccable courtesy, the stork gracefully endured the humiliating ordeal. Before departing, she cordially reciprocated the hospitality, extending an invitation for the fox to join her for dinner on the ensuing evening.

Promptly the following sunset, the expectant fox arrived at the stork's dwelling, anticipating a complimentary feast. To his profound chagrin, the meal was presented inside towering, slender flacons characterized by extremely narrow apertures. The stork effortlessly inserted her long bill into the elongated neck, extracting succulent morsels of food with complete satisfaction. Conversely, the fox found it physically impossible to introduce even his snout into the vessel, reducing him to fruitlessly licking the exterior glaze.

Humiliated and famished, the defeated prankster slunk away into the shadows, forced to acknowledge that his malicious scheme had been ingeniously turned against him.

True mutual respect requires considering the unique circumstances of others, whereas those who deliberately subject their peers to embarrassment must expect equivalent retribution.

## 2. Target Level: Eiken Grade 2 (英検2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~3 to 4 minutes. Academic discourse, complex participial structures, formal vocabulary, and nuanced transitions.*

In the realm of animal diplomacy, a notoriously manipulative fox engaged in a superficial alliance with an austere stork, preserving a facade of social harmony that concealed deep-seated psychological antagonism. Seeking to assert intellectual superiority through interpersonal humiliation, the vulpine host orchestrated a banquet at his subterranean domicile, framing the event as a celebration of mutual rapport.

Upon the guest's punctual arrival, the host unveiled an aromatic gastronomic concoction decanted exclusively across broad, low-rimmed ceramic salvers. This deliberate aesthetic configuration was calibrated to exploit anatomical divergence: while the host's expansive oral anatomy facilitated effortless ingestion, the stork's rigid, elongated rostrum proved structurally incompatible with horizontal liquid planes. Repeated attempts to harvest the broth merely wetted the avian bill, leaving the visitor in a state of acute caloric deprivation. Masking sadistic amusement behind disingenuous hospitality, the fox articulated mock sympathy, attributing her involuntary abstinence to culinary fastidiousness.

Repressing instinctive indignation in favor of strategic retaliation, the stork preserved an attitude of unblemished equanimity. Prior to concluding the visitation, she formally reciprocated the gesture, extending an invitation for an evening banquet at her arboreal residence on the subsequent evening.

Drawn by the prospect of unearned indulgence, the expectant fox arrived at the designated hour, anticipating lavish culinary hospitality. To his acute discomfiture, the meal had been decanted into towering, narrow-necked amphorae characterized by constricted apertures. With elegant mechanical precision, the avian hostess introduced her slender beak deep into the vessel's vertical interior, extracting succulent provisions with total autonomy. In stark contrast, the fox's cranial anatomy presented an insurmountable geometric impediment; unable to penetrate the aperture, the guest was reduced to licking microscopic residue from the external ceramic perimeter.

Thoroughly discomfited and physically depleted, the schemer executed a humiliating retreat, compelled to recognize that his pedagogical humiliation had been reciprocated with mathematical symmetry.

Authentic interpersonal ethics necessitate empathetic accommodation of diverse physiological and contextual realities, whereas institutionalizing malicious exclusion invites retaliatory retribution of identical proportion.

## 3. Classic Original English Text (イソップ寓話 クラシック原文)
*Townsend / Jacobs Edition (Public Domain)*

At one time the Fox and the Stork seemed to be on very good terms and invited each other to dinner. First the Fox invited the Stork, and served nothing but soup in a very shallow dish. The Fox lapped it up with ease, but the Stork could only wet the end of her long bill in it, and left the meal as hungry as when she came. "I am sorry," said the Fox, "that the soup is not to your liking." The Stork thanked him and invited him to dine with her in return. When the Fox arrived, the Stork served the meal in a tall jar with a narrow neck. The Stork easily inserted her long bill and enjoyed the food, while the Fox could only lick the outside of the jar.

One bad turn deserves another.

### 🏛️ 【英検2級レベルで読み解く！クラシック原文の重要構文・英文法ノート】
1. **1. 分詞構文（Participle Clause: 付帯状況・理由）**
   - **原文**: *"The Hare, believing her assertion to be simply impossible, agreed to the proposal..."*
   - **解説**: 「〜と信じ込んでいたので」という理由を表す分詞構文。英検2級の長文読解・ライティングの最頻出構文です。
1. **2. 古典的仮定法現在（Subjunctive Mood）**
   - **原文**: *"Though you be swift as the wind, I will beat you in a race."*
   - **解説**: 譲歩節 `Though` の中で原形 `be` を用いる古典的な仮定法現在（現代英語の `Though you are...`）。格調高い古典の響きを持ちます。
1. **3. 過去分詞の後置修飾（Postpositive Participle）**
   - **原文**: *"On the day appointed for the race the two started together."*
   - **解説**: `appointed`（定められた・指定された）が直前の名詞 `the day` を後ろから修飾する構文です。

### 📜 【クラシック原文の重要語彙・古風表現（Classic Antique Vocabulary）】
- **retort** [動] 2級 **（機転を利かせて）言い返す、反論する**（現代の reply sharp/wittily に相当）
- **assertion** [名] 2級/準1級 **主張、断言**（動詞 assert の名詞形。2級長文定番）
- **wayside** [名] 2級 **道端、沿道**（by the wayside（道端に、途中で））
- **fatigue** [名] 2級 **（心身の）極度の疲労、疲れ**（tiredness の上位フォーマル語）

## 4. Japanese Translation (日本語訳)

動物たちの外交関係において、悪名高い策略家のキツネが、厳格なツルと表向きの同盟を結び、根深い心理的敵意を覆い隠す社会的一和の仮面を維持していました。対人関係における屈辱を通じて知的な優位性を主張しようと企み、このキツネの主人は自らの地下の住居で宴会を画策し、その催しを相互の親睦の祝宴として仕立て上げました。

客が時間通りに到着すると、主人は縁の低い広い陶器の平皿にのみ注がれた香り高い料理を披露しました。この計算された美的な配置は、解剖学的相違を悪用するよう綿密に設計されていました。主人の広い口腔構造が苦労のない摂取を促進した一方で、ツルの硬く細長いクチバシは水平な液面とは構造的に適合不可能であることが判明したのです。スープを採取しようとする度重なる試みは鳥類のクチバシを濡らしたにすぎず、訪問者を深刻な熱量剥奪（飢餓）状態のままに放置しました。加虐的な面白がりを不誠実なもてなしの背後に覆い隠し、キツネは彼女の不本意な断食を料理の好みの気難しさのせいにしながら、芝居がかった同情を口にしました。

戦略的報復を期して本能的な憤りを抑え込み、ツルは非の打ちどころのない冷静沈着さを維持しました。訪問を終える前に、彼女は公式にその返礼を行い、翌日の夕方に自らの住まいで行われる晩餐会への招待状を差し出しました。

労せずして得られる贅沢の予感に惹かれ、期待に胸を膨らませたキツネは指定された時刻に到着し、豪勢なもてなしを予期していました。彼が痛烈な困惑を覚えたことには、食事は狭隘な開口部を特徴とする背の高い細首のアンフォラ（両取っ手付きの壺）に注がれていました。優雅な機械的正確さをもって、鳥類の女主人は自らの細いクチバシを容器の垂直な内部の深みへと差し入れ、完全な自律性をもって滋味豊かな食糧を抽出しました。これと著しい対照をなし、キツネの頭蓋骨の解剖学的構造は乗り越えがたい幾何学的障害を提示しました。開口部を貫通することができず、客は外側の陶器の周縁部から微小な残留物を舐めることへと零落させられたのです。

徹底的に面目を失い肉体的に衰弱したこの謀略家は、屈辱的な退却を敢行し、自らの加虐的な侮辱が数学的な対称性をもって返礼されたのだと認めざるを得ませんでした。

**教訓**: 真の対人倫理は、多様な生理学的および文脈的現実に対する共感的な配慮を必要とする。他者を排除する悪意ある計略を制度化することは、全く同等の比率による報復的な応報を招くことになる。

### 【出題頻出語彙・熟語ノート】
- **calibrate** [動] （機器・計画など）を精密に調整する、測定する
- **divergence** [名] 相違、分岐、逸脱（動詞 *diverge*）
- **incompatible** [形] 相容れない、両立しない、不適合な（*incompatible with...*）
- **equanimity** [名] 平静、落ち着き、沈着（*with equanimity*「冷静に」）
- **discomfiture** [名] 困惑、狼狽、当惑（動詞 *discomfit*）
- **impediment** [名] 障害、妨げ（動詞 *impede*）
- **necessitate** [動] （〜を）必要とする、不可欠にする（*require* の堅い語彙）

### 【文法・構文ポイント】
1. **名詞主語と動詞 [necessitate]（〜が…を必然的に要求する）**
   - 2級本文: *"Authentic interpersonal ethics necessitate empathetic accommodation of diverse realities..."*
   - ポイント: 「AのためにはBが必要である」という条件・要件を、無生物主語構文で極めて格調高く提示します。
2. **対比を鮮明にする分詞構文 [in stark contrast]**
   - 2級本文: *"In stark contrast, the fox's cranial anatomy presented an insurmountable geometric impediment..."*
   - ポイント: 「著しい対照をなして」という前置詞句を主文の冒頭に配置し、二者の置かれた立場の落差を劇的に浮き彫りにします。
