## 1. Benchmark Level: Eiken Pre-2 (英検準2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~2 to 2.5 minutes. Advanced vocabulary and structured clauses.*

A venerable matriarch of a frog colony resided alongside a tranquil marsh, notorious among her peers for an inflated sense of self-importance. One sultry afternoon, a colossal ox strolled down to the water's edge to drink, inadvertently crushing several small plants and shaking the surrounding soil with his ponderous steps.

Witnessing this display of sheer magnitude, the juvenile frogs fled in terror to their mother. "Mother, we have just encountered an incomprehensible behemoth!" they cried in trembling tones. "His limbs were as stout as oak trunks, and his stature rivaled a small mountain!"

Incapable of tolerating the notion that any entity could overshadow her own prestige, the conceited amphibian dismissed their description with contemptuous disdain. "You are merely exaggerating," she scoffed. "With a modest exertion of willpower, I can readily distend my physique to match that ungainly quadrupeds."

Taking a profound inhalation, she stretched her elastic hide, puffing her torso into an awkward sphere. "Behold!" she proclaimed proudly. "Does the intruder surpass this magnitude?"

"By an immense margin, Mother!" her offspring exclaimed candidly. "Your present dimension is negligible in comparison to that monster!"

Infuriated by their contradiction, the obstinate mother redoubled her efforts. Drawing vast quantities of atmosphere into her lungs, she expanded her membranes to a perilous degree, straining her abdominal tissues beyond their natural elasticity. "Surely, I have now achieved parity!" she wheezed through clenched jaws.

"Cease this madness, Mother!" pleaded her terrified offspring. "Even if you were to rupture your own flesh, you could never equal the ox!"

Deaf to their desperate supplications and dominated entirely by irrational narcissism, the delusional creature drew one final, violent breath. Incapable of withstanding the immense internal pressure, her fragile body abruptly exploded with a sickening snap.

Pretentious vanity that refuses to acknowledge natural limitations will inevitably culminate in catastrophic self-annihilation.

## 2. Target Level: Eiken Grade 2 (英検2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~3 to 4 minutes. Academic discourse, complex participial structures, formal vocabulary, and nuanced transitions.*

Within a riparian wetland ecosystem, a matriarchal amphibian exercised informal dominance over her cohort, driven by an acute manifestation of megalomania and unyielding narcissistic entitlement. Her psychological stability was fundamentally contingent upon maintaining an unchallenged monopoly on local admiration. Consequently, when an immense herbivorous bovine—an ox of gigantic proportions—lumbered to the littoral margin to quench his metabolic thirst, the seismic impact of his physical mass destabilized her carefully cultivated regime of prestige.

Terrified by this visual encounter with biological magnitude, the juvenile offspring sought sanctuary beside their parent, breathlessly recounting the phenomenon. They depicted an organism of titanic physical dimensions whose mass displaced the marshland waters and whose hooves pulverized geological formations. Incapable of reconciling this objective reality with her pathological self-concept, the matriarch immediately exhibited cognitive dissonance, rationalizing that perceived biological disparities could be surmounted through sheer mechanical inflation.

In an absurd attempt to fabricate biometric parity, she initiated forced pulmonary expansion, distending her dermis and subcutaneous tissues through hyperventilation. "Observe my volumetric adaptation," she demanded imperiously. "Does this synthetic expansion approximate the dimensions of the intruder?"

"Your current volume represents a statistically negligible fraction of that organism," the progeny responded with unvarnished empirical candor. "Any attempt to bridge this evolutionary chasm through volumetric inflation is biologically delusional."

Provoked to paroxysms of narcissistic rage by this factual contradiction, the creature accelerated her hyper-inflationary regimen. Inhaling atmospheric gases with desperate vigor, she pressurized her coelomic cavity to critical structural thresholds, inducing severe micro-tearing across her vascularized membrane. Her circulatory and respiratory apparatuses bordered on catastrophic structural failure, yet her compulsive fixation upon simulated equality precluded rational self-preservation. "Behold!" she gasped through severely distorted vocal cords. "I have definitively eradicated the disparity!"

"Desist immediately, Mother!" the juvenile observers shrieked in horror. "Biological constraints are immutable; structural compromise is imminent!"

Subordinating somatic survival to the desperate preservation of an untenable illusion, the delusional organism forced a culminating volume of compressed atmosphere into her compromised anatomy. Surpassing the tensile strength of her biological tissues, the hydrostatic pressure triggered explosive structural decompression. In an instant, the organism disintegrated into visceral fragments, leaving her stunned offspring amidst the silent marsh.

Unfettered megalomania that rejects biological reality inevitably precipitates catastrophic structural collapse; attempting to simulate parity with the insurmountable terminates in complete self-annihilation.

## 3. Classic Original English Text (イソップ寓話 クラシック原文)
*Townsend / Jacobs Edition (Public Domain)*

An Ox, drinking at a pool, stepped on a family of young frogs and crushed one of them to death. When the mother frog came home, she asked where her little one was. "A huge beast came by," said one of the brothers, "and crushed him under his big foot." "How big was he?" asked the mother frog, puffing herself out. "Was he as big as this?" "Oh, much bigger than that!" replied the young frog. The mother puffed herself out more and more, asking, "Was he this big?" "Cease your puffing, mother," said the son, "for if you burst yourself, you will never equal the beast." But the foolish frog tried once more, blew herself up even bigger, and burst!

Self-conceit may lead to self-destruction.

### 🏛️ 【英検2級レベルで読み解く！クラシック原文の重要構文・英文法ノート】
1. **1. 分詞構文（Participle Clause: 付帯状況・理由）**
   - **原文**: *"The Hare, believing her assertion to be simply impossible, agreed to the proposal..."*
   - **解説**: 「〜と信じ込んでいたので」という理由を表す分詞構文。英検2級の長文読解・ライティングの最頻出構文です。
1. **2. 古典的仮定法現在（Subjunctive Mood）**
   - **原文**: *"Though you be swift as the wind, I will beat you in a race."*
   - **解説**: 譲歩節 `Though` の中で原形 `be` を用いる古典的な仮定法現在（現代英語の `Though you are...`）。格調高い古典の響きを持ちます。
1. **3. 過去分詞の後置修飾（Postpositive Participle）**
   - **原文**: *"On the day appointed for the race the two started together."*
   - **解説**: `appointed`（定められた・指定された）が直前の名詞 `the day` を後ろから修飾する構文です。

### 📜 【クラシック原文の重要語彙・古風表現（Classic Antique Vocabulary）】
- **retort** [動] 2級 **（機転を利かせて）言い返す、反論する**（現代の reply sharp/wittily に相当）
- **assertion** [名] 2級/準1級 **主張、断言**（動詞 assert の名詞形。2級長文定番）
- **wayside** [名] 2級 **道端、沿道**（by the wayside（道端に、途中で））
- **fatigue** [名] 2級 **（心身の）極度の疲労、疲れ**（tiredness の上位フォーマル語）

## 4. Japanese Translation (日本語訳)

河川沿いの湿地生態系の中で、ある長老格の両生類が、誇大妄想狂と不屈の自己愛的な特権意識の鋭い表出に駆り立てられ、同胞たちに対して非公式の支配力を行使していました。彼女の心理的安定は、地域社会における称賛の独占を脅かされないまま維持することに根本的に依存していました。その結果、巨大な体躯を誇る草食性の牛が代謝上の渇きを癒やすために水辺へと重々しく歩み寄り、その物理的質量の地震のような衝撃が彼女の丹念に築き上げた威信の体制を不安定化させた時、危機が勃発したのです。

生物学的な巨大さとのこの視覚的遭遇に恐れおののき、幼い子孫たちは親の元へと避難し、息を切らしながらその現象を物語りました。彼らは、その質量が湿地の水を押し退け、その蹄が地質学的構造を粉砕するような、巨大な物理的次元を持つ有機体を描写しました。この客観的現実と自らの病的な自己概念とを調和させることができなかった母ガエルは、即座に認知的不協和を露呈し、知覚された生物学的格差は純粋な機械的膨張によって克服可能であると合理化しました。

生体計測上の同等性を捏造しようとする不条理な試みの中で、彼女は強制的な肺の拡張を開始し、過呼吸によって自らの真皮および皮下組織を肥大化させました。「私の体積的適応を観察しなさい」と彼女は横柄に命じました。「この人工的な拡張は侵入者の寸法に近似しているかい？」

「現在のお母さんの体積は、あの有機体の統計学的に無視できる割合を表しているにすぎません」と子孫たちは飾り気のない経験的率直さをもって答えました。「体積の膨張によってこの進化の深淵を埋めようとするいかなる試みも、生物学的な妄想です。」

この事実に基づく反論によって自己愛の激怒の衝動へと駆り立てられたこの生き物は、自らの過膨張のプログラムを加速させました。必死の力で大気ガスを吸い込み、彼女は体腔を危機的な構造閾値まで加圧し、血管の通った皮膜全体に深刻な微小断裂を引き起こしました。彼女の循環器系および呼吸器系の器官は破滅的な構造崩壊の寸前に達していましたが、シミュレートされた平等への強迫的な執着が理性的な自己保身を排除しました。「見なさい！」と彼女は激しく歪んだ声帯の隙間から喘ぎました。「私は決定的に格差を根絶したぞ！」

「今すぐやめて、お母さん！」と幼い観察者たちは恐怖に金切り声を上げました。「生物学的制約は不変です。構造的破綻は目前です！」

維持不可能な幻想の必死の維持に肉体的な生存を従属させ、この妄想的な有機体は、損傷した自らの肉体へと限界を決定づける体積の圧縮大気を無理やり流し込みました。自らの生体組織の引張強度を超えた流体静力学的圧力が、爆発的な構造減圧を引き起こしました。一瞬にして、有機体は内臓の破片へと四散し、呆然とする子孫たちを静まり返った湿地の中に取り残したのです。

**教訓**: 生物学的現実を拒絶する野放図な誇大妄想は、必然的に破滅的な構造崩壊を招く。克服不可能な相手との同等性を模倣しようとする企ては、完全な自己消滅へと至る。

### 【出題頻出語彙・熟語ノート】
- **megalomania** [名] 誇大妄想、権力欲（形容詞 *megalomaniac*）
- **contingent on/upon...** [熟] 〜に依存している、〜次第である（＝*dependent on*）
- **cognitive dissonance** [名] 認知的不協和（心理学重要概念）
- **surmount** [動] （困難・格差など）を克服する、乗り越える
- **chasm** [名] 深い裂け目、隔絶、大きな隔たり（*bridge a chasm*）
- **immutable** [形] 不変の、容易に変わらない（反義語：*mutable*）
- **unfettered** [形] 拘束されない、野放図な（動詞 *fetter*「足枷をはめる」）

### 【文法・構文ポイント】
1. **動詞句構文 [bridge a chasm]（深淵を埋める、架け橋となる）**
   - 2級本文: *"Any attempt to bridge this evolutionary chasm through volumetric inflation is biologically delusional."*
   - ポイント: 「埋めがたい格差や隔たりを解消する」ことを比喩的に表す極めて格調の高いコロケーションです。
2. **分詞による動因描写 [Surpassing the tensile strength ..., the pressure triggered ...]**
   - 2級本文: *"Surpassing the tensile strength of her biological tissues, the hydrostatic pressure triggered explosive structural decompression."*
   - ポイント: 材料の物理的限界を超えたという前置分詞構文によって、続く崩壊の必然性を科学的・論理的に導出します。
