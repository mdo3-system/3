## 1. Benchmark Level: Eiken Grade 3 (英検3級レベル本文)
*Read in ~1.5 to 2 minutes. Baseline vocabulary and basic syntax.*

An eagle swooped down from a high cliff with incredible speed and seized a small lamb in its powerful talons, carrying it effortlessly toward its nest.

A foolish jackdaw, which had been observing the magnificent feat from a nearby branch, was filled with foolish envy. Convinced that he possessed equal strength and skill, he resolved to imitate the king of birds.

Circling high above the pasture, the jackdaw selected the largest and heaviest ram in the flock. He plunged down recklessly and landed upon the beast's back. However, when he attempted to lift the enormous animal into the air, he discovered that he lacked the strength to move it even an inch.

To make matters worse, his sharp claws became hopelessly entangled in the thick, curly fleece. Despite his frantic flapping, he could not free himself.

The shepherd, who had witnessed the entire scene, walked over and captured the struggling bird without difficulty. After clipping the jackdaw's wings, the shepherd brought him home as a plaything for his children.

"What kind of creature is this?" the curious children inquired.

"If you ask him," the shepherd chuckled, "he claims to be an eagle; but if you judge him by his actions, he is merely a foolish jackdaw."

Imitating the powerful without possessing their strength will inevitably lead to humiliation and disaster.

## 2. Target Level: Eiken Pre-2 (英検準2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~2.5 to 3.5 minutes. Advanced vocabulary, cause-and-effect transitions, participial constructions, and formal discourse.*

Descending precipitously from an inaccessible alpine precipice, an apex eagle captured an unblemished lamb with breathtaking precision, hoisting the livestock airborne through sheer muscular potency. Witnessing this awe-inspiring demonstration of aerial supremacy, a vainglorious jackdaw experienced an overwhelming surge of irrational ambition. Deluded by superficial observation into believing that physical stature was negligible in the execution of predatory feats, the diminutive corvid resolved to duplicate the raptor's triumph.

Surveying the pastoral meadow beneath, the ambitious pretender targeted the most colossal and formidable ram in the flock, erroneously presuming that greater quarry would yield greater renown. Accelerating into a steep dive with a noisy flurry of feathers, he descended violently upon the beast's woolly spine, sinking his curved talons deeply into the matted fleece.

However, the profound disparity between his grandiose aspiration and physiological capacity instantly manifested itself. When the jackdaw strained frantically to elevate the massive quadruped into the atmosphere, the unyielding ram barely acknowledged the disturbance, continuing to graze placidly. Furthermore, the bird's slender claws became inextricably entwined within the dense, convoluted fibers of the wool. Entrapped by his own miscalculated assault, his hysterical fluttering merely tightened the knots.

Having observed the ludicrous spectacle with amused detachment, the attentive shepherd approached and effortlessly apprehended the immobilised bird. Subjecting the captive to humiliating wing-clipping to prevent escape, the herdsman presented the prisoner as a curious novelty for his household.

When his children inquired regarding the species of this bizarre pet, the shepherd delivered a pointed verdict: "In accordance with his inflated delusions, he masquerades as an imperial eagle; nevertheless, judged by the reality of his limited competence, he remains an utterly insignificant jackdaw."

Blindly emulating the accomplishments of superior beings without assessing one's innate limitations inevitably invites catastrophic humiliation.

## 3. Classic Original English Text (イソップ寓話 クラシック原文)
*Townsend / Jacobs Edition (Public Domain)*

An Eagle flew down from a high mountain crag, and carried away a Lamb in his talons. A Jackdaw saw him, and thought that he could do the same. So he flew down upon a Ram with a great whirr, and fastened his claws in the fleece; but when he tried to fly away again, he found he could not move the Ram, and his claws were so entangled in the wool that he could not get away. 

The Shepherd saw what had happened, and ran up and caught the bird. He clipped his wings, and took him home in the evening to his children. 

"What bird is this?" they cried. 

"Well," said the Shepherd, "if you ask him, he'll tell you he is an Eagle; but I know he is only a Jackdaw."

Do not let your vanity lead you to attempt what is beyond your power.

### 🏛️ 【英検2級レベルで読み解く！クラシック原文の重要構文・英文法ノート】
1. **1. 分詞構文（Participle Clause: 付帯状況・理由）**
   - **原文**: *"The Hare, believing her assertion to be simply impossible, agreed to the proposal..."*
   - **解説**: 「〜と信じ込んでいたので」という理由を表す分詞構文。英検2級の長文読解・ライティングの最頻出構文です。
1. **2. 古典的仮定法現在（Subjunctive Mood）**
   - **原文**: *"Though you be swift as the wind, I will beat you in a race."*
   - **解説**: 譲歩節 `Though` の中で原形 `be` を用いる古典的な仮定法現在（現代英語の `Though you are...`）。格調高い古典の響きを持ちます。
1. **3. 過去分詞の後置修飾（Postpositive Participle）**
   - **原文**: *"On the day appointed for the race the two started together."*
   - **解説**: `appointed`（定められた・指定された）が直前の名詞 `the day` を後ろから修飾する構文です。

### 📜 【クラシック原文の重要語彙・古風表現（Classic Antique Vocabulary）】
- **retort** [動] 2級 **（機転を利かせて）言い返す、反論する**（現代の reply sharp/wittily に相当）
- **assertion** [名] 2級/準1級 **主張、断言**（動詞 assert の名詞形。2級長文定番）
- **wayside** [名] 2級 **道端、沿道**（by the wayside（道端に、途中で））
- **fatigue** [名] 2級 **（心身の）極度の疲労、疲れ**（tiredness の上位フォーマル語）

## 4. Japanese Translation (日本語訳)

人が立ち入ることのできない高山の断崖から急降下した頂点捕食者のワシは、息をのむような正確さで無傷の子羊を捕らえ、純粋な筋力によってその家畜を空中に持ち上げました。この空の覇権の畏怖すべきデモンストレーションを目撃した自惚れの強いカラスは、理性を失った野望の圧倒的な高まりを経験しました。表面的な観察に惑わされ、捕食の偉業を達成する上で身体の大きさなど取るに足らないと思い込んだこの小さなカラスは、猛禽の勝利を自らも再現しようと決意しました。

眼下の牧歌的な草原を見渡した野心的な偽者は、群れの中で最も巨大で頑強な雄羊を標的に定め、獲物が大きければ大きいほどより大きな名声が得られるだろうと誤って推測しました。羽毛の騒々しい羽ばたきと共に急降下へ加速した彼は、その獣の羊毛に覆われた背骨の上に激しく舞い降り、もつれた羊毛の中に曲がった爪を深く食い込ませました。

しかしながら、彼の壮大な野望と実際の生理的能力との間の甚だしい落差は、瞬時に自らを現しました。カラスがその巨大な四足動物を大気中へ持ち上げようと必死に力を振り絞ったものの、びくともしない雄羊はその邪魔にほとんど気づきもせず、穏やかに草を食み続けました。さらに悪いことに、鳥の細い爪は羊毛の濃密で入り組んだ繊維の中に解けないほど絡まり合ってしまったのです。自らの誤った計算による強襲によって囚われの身となった彼のヒステリックな羽ばたきは、ただ結び目をきつく締め上げるだけでした。

この滑稽な見世物を面白がりながら眺めていた注意深い羊飼いが歩み寄り、動けなくなった鳥を難なく捕獲しました。逃亡を防ぐために屈辱的な羽切りを施した後、羊飼いは自分の家族のための珍しい見世物としてその捕虜を差し出しました。

子供たちがこの奇妙なペットの種族について尋ねたとき、羊飼いは的を射た宣告を下しました。「自らの肥大化した妄想によれば、彼は帝国のワシを気取っている。それにもかかわらず、その限られた能力の現実によって判断されるならば、彼は全くもって取るに足らないカラスに過ぎないのだ」

**教訓**: 自らの生来の限界を見極めることなく、優れた者の業績を盲目的に模倣することは、必然的に壊滅的な屈辱を招く。

### 【出題頻出語彙・熟語ノート】
- **supremacy** [名] 最高位、覇権、至高（形容詞 *supreme*）
- **duplicate** [動] 〜を複製する、再現する（名詞としても頻出）
- **disparity** [名] 相違、格差、不均衡（*gap / difference*）
- **grandiose** [形] 壮大な、気取った、誇大妄想の
- **manifest** [動] 〜を明らかにする、現れる（形容詞：明白な）
- **entwine** [動] 〜を絡み合わせる、巻き付かせる
- **ludicrous** [形] 滑稽な、ばかげた（*ridiculous* の類語）
- **masquerade** [動] （〜の）仮面をかぶる、〜を装う（*masquerade as*）
- **competence** [名] 能力、適性、力量（形容詞 *competent*）
- **emulate** [動] 〜を見習う、競い合う、模倣する

### 【文法・構文ポイント】
1. **分詞構文による条件・判断 [Judged by ...]**
   - 準2級本文: *"...nevertheless, judged by the reality of his limited competence, he remains an utterly insignificant jackdaw."*
   - ポイント: 「〜によって判断されると」という慣用的な分詞構文（Judged from / Judged by）。客観的基準に基づいた評価を下す際の定番表現です。
2. **抽象名詞主語と動詞 [invite + 望ましくない結果]（〜を招く）**
   - 準2級本文: *"...without assessing one's innate limitations inevitably invites catastrophic humiliation."*
   - ポイント: `invite` は「招待する」だけでなく、「危険・批判・屈辱などを（自ら）招き寄せる」という意味で用いられます。英作文の教訓部分で非常に好まれる表現です。
