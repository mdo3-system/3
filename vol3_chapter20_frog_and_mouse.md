## 1. Benchmark Level: Eiken Pre-2 (英検準2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~2 to 2.5 minutes. Advanced vocabulary and structured clauses.*

A trusting terrestrial mouse established an amicable relationship with an amphibious frog, completely oblivious to the malevolent malice festering within his associate's mind. Harboring a perverse hostility toward creatures inhabiting dry land, the treacherous amphibian conceived a premeditated plot to assassinate his companion.

Under the fraudulent pretext of introducing the mouse to the wonders of aquatic life, the frog proposed a joint excursion across a tranquil lagoon. To ensure that they remained united during their journey, the conniving instigator bound one of the mouse's hind limbs securely to his own with a robust reed fiber. Intimidated by the unfamiliar aquatic environment yet reluctant to doubt his friend's sincerity, the apprehensive mouse reluctantly acquiesced to the arrangement.

Initially, the frog swam gently across the surface, disarming any lingering reservations. However, upon reaching the deepest section of the pool, the traitor executed his heinous design. With sudden violence, he submerged abruptly, relentlessly dragging the non-swimming rodent beneath the dark water. Despite thrashing desperately in a futile bid for survival, the suffocated mammal rapidly succumbed to asphyxiation, his lifeless corpse subsequently bobbing to the surface.

Gleeful over the successful consummation of his treachery, the amphibian floated beneath the corpse, savoring his cruelty. However, retributive justice struck from the heavens with terrifying velocity. A soaring kite scanning the water for carrion intercepted the conspicuous silhouette. Plunging like an arrow, the raptor clamped its formidable talons onto the floating carcass and ascended rapidly into the stratosphere.

Because the submerged traitor remained inextricably linked to his victim by the indestructible cord, the frog was involuntarily hauled into the atmosphere alongside the deceased rodent. Realizing his inescapable doom, the shrieking amphibian was carried away to a desolate precipice, where the raptor expeditiously devoured both conspirator and victim in a single feast.

Perfidious cruelty contains the seeds of its own destruction, ensuring that those who orchestrate the annihilation of the innocent inevitably bind themselves to identical ruin.

## 2. Target Level: Eiken Grade 2 (英検2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~3 to 4 minutes. Academic discourse, complex participial structures, formal vocabulary, and nuanced transitions.*

In an ecological interface bridging aquatic and terrestrial habitats, a naive terrestrial rodent initiated an interspecies association with an amphibious anuran. Psychologically predisposed to goodwill, the mammalian partner remained entirely oblivious to the sociopathic malice harbored by his counterpart. Resenting terrestrial sovereignty and harboring a morbid compulsion to demonstrate aquatic supremacy, the duplicitous frog formulated a premeditated conspiracy designed to liquidate his associate under the guise of pastoral camaraderie.

To operationalize his homicidal design, the amphibian fabricated an enticing narrative of botanical tourism, inviting the unsuspecting rodent to inspect submerged benthic ecosystems. Overcoming the mammal's instinctive hydrophobia, the instigator proposed a binding ligature—a durable botanical cord fashioned from fibrous reeds—affixed securely between their respective limbs, disingenuously framing the apparatus as a precautionary safety tether. Paralyzed by the dread of appearing ungrateful and subverting his rational anxieties to social compliance, the gullible rodent capitulated to the lethal entanglement.

Upon navigating beyond the littoral shallows into pelagic depths, the conspirator initiated the terminal phase of his scheme. Abruptly diving toward the benthic sediment, the frog mobilized his specialized propulsion to drag the buoyant, non-adapted mammal beneath the water line. The rodent's desperate physiological convulsions proved utterly futile against fluid dynamics; within minutes, acute hypoxia induced respiratory collapse, resulting in terminal drowning. As cellular respiration ceased, the buoyant corpse bobbed inertly upon the liquid surface, anchored invisibly to the lurking perpetrator beneath.

While the perfidious anuran lingered beneath the floating cadaver, indulging in premature self-congratulation, apex aerial surveillance altered the trajectory of the encounter. An opportunistic raptor—a red kite surveying the lagoon for biomass—detected the conspicuous horizontal displacement of the buoyant mammal. Executing a steep parabolic dive, the bird of prey embedded its razor-sharp talons into the floating mammalian flesh, immediately initiating high-velocity vertical ascension.

Crucially, the physical ligature that had functioned as an instrument of deliberate murder now transformed into an inescapable conduit of retributive doom. Inextricably anchored to the ascending carcass, the submerged amphibian was violently yanked across the surface tension and catapulted into the atmosphere. Incapable of severing the synthetic bond, the screaming anuran was transported to a precipitous cliffside redoubt, where the raptor systematically dismembered and devoured both the innocent casualty and the treacherous architect of the catastrophe.

Malicious conspiracies engineered against the vulnerable contain an intrinsic boomerang dynamic; those who bind others to unjustified destruction inadvertently construct the precise mechanisms that guarantee their own catastrophic demise.

## 3. Classic Original English Text (イソップ寓話 クラシック原文)
*Townsend / Jacobs Edition (Public Domain)*

A Mouse became friends with a Frog, but the Frog harbored wicked intentions. One day, the Frog tied the Mouse's leg to his own with a strong thread, promising to show him the sights of the pond. The Frog swam into the middle of the pond and dived beneath the surface, dragging the helpless Mouse down until he drowned. While the Mouse's body floated on the water, a Hawk flying overhead spotted it. The Hawk swooped down, seized the dead Mouse, and carried him away. Because the Frog was still tied to the Mouse, the Hawk devoured both of them together.

Harm set, harm get.

### 🏛️ 【英検2級レベルで読み解く！クラシック原文の重要構文・英文法ノート】
1. **1. 分詞構文（Participle Clause: 付帯状況・理由）**
   - **原文**: *"The Hare, believing her assertion to be simply impossible, agreed to the proposal..."*
   - **解説**: 「〜と信じ込んでいたので」という理由を表す分詞構文。英検2級の長文読解・ライティングの最頻出構文です。
1. **2. 古典的仮定法現在（Subjunctive Mood）**
   - **原文**: *"Though you be swift as the wind, I will beat you in a race."*
   - **解説**: 譲歩節 `Though` の中で原形 `be` を用いる古典的な仮定法現在（現代英語の `Though you are...`）。格調高い古典の響きを持ちます。
1. **3. 過去分詞の後置修飾（Postpositive Participle）**
   - **原文**: *"On the day appointed for the race the two started together."*
   - **解説**: `appointed`（定められた・指定された）が直前の名詞 `the day` を後ろから修飾する構文です。

### 📜 【クラシック原文の重要語彙・古風表現（Classic Antique Vocabulary）】
- **retort** [動] 2級 **（機転を利かせて）言い返す、反論する**（現代の reply sharp/wittily に相当）
- **assertion** [名] 2級/準1級 **主張、断言**（動詞 assert の名詞形。2級長文定番）
- **wayside** [名] 2級 **道端、沿道**（by the wayside（道端に、途中で））
- **fatigue** [名] 2級 **（心身の）極度の疲労、疲れ**（tiredness の上位フォーマル語）

## 4. Japanese Translation (日本語訳)

水生と陸生の生息地をつなぐ生態学的境界領域において、世間知らずな陸生げっ歯類が、両生類の無尾目（カエル）と種を超えた交友関係を開始しました。心理的に善意を信じる傾向にあった哺乳類のパートナーは、相手が抱く反社会的な悪意にまったく気づいていませんでした。陸上の主権を妬み、水中の優越性を誇示したいという病的な強迫観念を抱いていたこの不実なカエルは、田園的な友情の仮面の下で相棒を抹殺するよう設計された計画的陰謀を企てました。

自らの殺害計画を実行に移すため、両生類は植物学的観光という魅力的な物語をでっち上げ、水没した底生生態系を視察するよう疑うことを知らないげっ歯類を招待しました。哺乳類の本能的な水恐怖症を克服させるため、首謀者は両者の足の間にしっかりと固定された強靭な植物性の紐――繊維質の葦から作られた耐久性のあるロープ――による結束を提案し、その装置を予防的な安全命綱として不誠実にも位置づけました。恩知らずと思われることへの恐怖に麻痺し、自らの理性的な不安を社会的従属に屈服させた騙されやすいげっ歯類は、その致命的なもつれ合いに降伏したのです。

沿岸の浅瀬を越えて水深の深い遠泳域へと航行するやいなや、共謀者は自らの計画の最終段階を開始しました。底生の堆積物に向かって突然潜水し、カエルは自らの特殊な推進力を動員して、浮力があり水に適応していない哺乳類を水面下へと引きずり込みました。げっ歯類の必死の生理学的痙攣は、流体力学の前には完全に無益でした。数分以内に、急性低酸素症が呼吸崩壊を誘発し、溺死という結末をもたらしました。細胞呼吸が停止すると、浮かぶ死骸は液体の水面の上に不活性に浮かび上がり、目に見えない形で下方に潜む犯人へと固定されていました。

不実なカエルが浮かぶ死体の下に留まり、早すぎる自画自賛に浸っている最中、上空の頂点監視が遭遇の軌道を一変させました。生物資源を求めてラグーンを観察していた日和見主義の猛禽――アカトビ――が、浮かぶ哺乳類の目立つ水平方向の変位を探知しました。急峻な放物線を描く急降下を実行し、猛禽はその剃刀のように鋭い爪を浮かぶ哺乳類の肉体に食い込ませ、即座に高速度の垂直上昇を開始しました。

決定的なことに、計画的殺人の道具として機能していた物理的な結束が、今や逃れられない報復の破滅の導管へと変貌しました。上昇する死骸へと不可分に固定された水中の両生類は、表面張力を越えて激しく引きずり出され、大気中へと放り投げられました。自ら結んだ人工的な絆を切断することができず、金切り声を上げるカエルは険しい崖の上の砦へと運ばれ、そこで猛禽は、無実の犠牲者とその大惨事の狡猾な設計者の両方を体系的に解体し、貪り食ったのです。

**教訓**: 無防備な者に対して仕掛けられた悪意ある陰謀は、内在的なブーメランの力学を内包している。不当な破滅へと他者を縛り付ける者は、意図せずして自らの破滅的な終末を保証するまさにその機構を構築することになる。

### 【文法・構文ポイント】
1. **動名詞による主語と帰結 [Binding others constructs ...]**
   - 2級本文: *"those who bind others to unjustified destruction inadvertently construct the precise mechanisms that guarantee their own catastrophic demise."*
   - ポイント: 「他者を束縛することが、自らの破滅の装置を構築する」という逆説的帰結を、動詞 `construct` と関係代名詞 `that guarantee...` で堅固に組み上げます。
2. **名詞の比喩的対比 [instrument of murder transformed into conduit of doom]**
   - 2級本文: *"the physical ligature that had functioned as an instrument of deliberate murder now transformed into an inescapable conduit of retributive doom."*
   - ポイント: 関係代名詞節によって以前の機能を明示し、主動詞 `transformed into` で皮肉な役割の逆転を鮮烈に提示する高度な構文です。
