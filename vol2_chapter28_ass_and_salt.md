## 1. Benchmark Level: Eiken Grade 3 (英検3級レベル本文)
*Read in ~1.5 to 2 minutes. Baseline vocabulary and basic syntax.*

A merchant who dealt in salt loaded several heavy sacks onto his donkey and set out toward the market. On their journey, they arrived at a shallow stream which had to be crossed on foot.

While wading through the stream, the donkey accidentally stumbled over a slippery rock and fell into the water. By the time he struggled back to his feet, much of the salt had completely dissolved. Finding his burden remarkably lighter, the donkey trotted home with great satisfaction.

The following day, when they reached the same stream, the clever beast decided to repeat the experience. He intentionally collapsed into the water. As expected, the salt melted away, allowing him to finish his journey without any difficulty.

Realizing that he had been outsmarted by his animal, the merchant devised a clever plan to teach the donkey a lasting lesson.

On their next trip, instead of salt, the master packed the sacks with dry sponges. When they reached the middle of the stream, the foolish donkey deliberately dropped to his knees once more.

However, the sponges immediately absorbed an enormous amount of water. When the beast attempted to rise, the load had become hopelessly heavy. Crushed beneath the unbearable weight, the donkey barely managed to crawl out of the river.

Those who continually rely on petty tricks will eventually cause their own misfortune.

## 2. Target Level: Eiken Pre-2 (英検準2級レベル本文)
*Read in ~2.5 to 3.5 minutes. Advanced vocabulary, cause-and-effect transitions, participial constructions, and formal discourse.*

Engaged in the lucrative commerce of unrefined mineral salt, an enterprising merchant laded his pack donkey with substantial burlap sacks and initiated his customary transit toward urban markets. Their standard trade route necessitated traversing a shallow river characterized by rapid currents and algae-encrusted riverbed stones.

While negotiating the aquatic ford, the pack animal experienced an inadvertent loss of equilibrium, toppling sideways into the submerged boulders. By the time the drenched quadruped regained a stable footing, rapid chemical dissolution had eliminated the vast majority of the saline cargo. Astonished by the miraculous reduction in gravitational resistance, the donkey concluded the transit with unprecedented buoyancy and ease.

Erroneously extrapolating from this isolated phenomenon, the obstinate beast formulated a fallacious hypothesis: immersion in running water served as a universal mechanism for lightening burdens. Consequently, upon returning to the identical ford on subsequent expeditions, the beast executed a premeditated collapse into the stream, triumphantly reducing the merchant's profits while easing his own physical exertion.

Perceiving the fraudulent strategy orchestrating these recurrent accidents, the indignant merchant formulated a sophisticated pedagogical countermeasure. Prior to their subsequent expedition, he substituted the saline contents with porous, dehydrated marine sponges, ensuring the cargo remained deceptive in its initial lightweight state.

Upon arriving at the midstream ford, the complacent donkey enthusiastically enacted his rehearsed stratagem, submerging himself beneath the surface. However, rather than dissolving, the capillary microstructure of the sponges aggressively absorbed colossal volumes of liquid. When the beast attempted to elevate himself, he discovered to his utter horror that his burden had expanded exponentially into a lethal mass. Trapped beneath the saturated cargo, the gasping animal was nearly submerged before barely dragging his exhausted frame onto the opposite embankment.

Relying mechanically upon fraudulent shortcuts inevitably produces catastrophic miscalculations when underlying circumstances undergo a fundamental transformation.

## 3. Classic Original English Text (イソップ寓話 クラシック原文)
*Townsend / Jacobs Edition (Public Domain)*

A Pedlar was driving an Ass to the seashore to buy salt. On their return, they came to a stream, which they had to cross. The Ass slipped and fell into the middle of the stream, and the salt was melted by the water, so that when the Ass got up again he found his load very much lighter. He was very much pleased with this. 

The next time the Pedlar came to the same stream with a load of salt, the Ass intentionally fell down into the water. Again the salt melted, and he rose with a much lighter burden. 

The Pedlar saw through the trick, and next time loaded him with sponges instead of salt. When they came to the stream, the Ass fell down on purpose once again. But the sponges became soaked with water, and so heavy that the Ass was unable to rise, and was drowned.

The same trick will not work twice.

### 🏛️ 【英検2級レベルで読み解く！クラシック原文の重要構文・英文法ノート】
1. **1. 分詞構文（Participle Clause: 付帯状況・理由）**
   - **原文**: *"The Hare, believing her assertion to be simply impossible, agreed to the proposal..."*
   - **解説**: 「〜と信じ込んでいたので」という理由を表す分詞構文。英検2級の長文読解・ライティングの最頻出構文です。
1. **2. 古典的仮定法現在（Subjunctive Mood）**
   - **原文**: *"Though you be swift as the wind, I will beat you in a race."*
   - **解説**: 譲歩節 `Though` の中で原形 `be` を用いる古典的な仮定法現在（現代英語の `Though you are...`）。格調高い古典の響きを持ちます。
1. **3. 過去分詞の後置修飾（Postpositive Participle）**
   - **原文**: *"On the day appointed for the race the two started together."*
   - **解説**: `appointed`（定められた・指定された）が直前の名詞 `the day` を後ろから修飾する構文です。

- **retort** [動] 2級 **（機転を利かせて）言い返す、反論する**（現代の reply sharp/wittily に相当）

## 4. Japanese Translation (日本語訳)

精製されていない鉱物塩の利益の高い取引に従事していたある進取の気性に富む商人が、荷運びロバに大量の麻袋を積載し、都市の市場へ向けていつもの輸送を開始しました。彼らの標準的な交易ルートは、速い潮流と藻に覆われた川底の石を特徴とする浅い川を横断することを余儀なくさせていました。

浅瀬の渡河をこなしている最中、その使役動物は不意に平衡感覚を失い、水没した岩の上に横倒しに転倒しました。ずぶ濡れになった四足動物が安定した足場を取り戻す頃には、急速な化学的溶解によって塩の積載物の大部分が消滅していました。重力抵抗の奇跡的な減少に驚嘆したロバは、前例のない身軽さと容易さをもってその旅程を終えました。

この個別の現象から誤って過剰な一般化を導き出したその頑固な獣は、「流水への水没は荷物を軽くするための普遍的な仕組みとして機能する」という誤謬に満ちた仮説を構築してしまいました。その結果、その後の遠征で同一の浅瀬に戻るたびに、その獣は計画的な転倒を川の中で敢行し、自らの肉体的労苦を和らげる一方で、商人の利益を意気揚々と減少させたのです。

これらの頻発する事故を画策している詐欺的な戦略を見抜いた憤慨した商人は、高度に教育的な対抗策を策定しました。次回の遠征に先立ち、彼は塩の積荷を多孔質の乾燥した天然海綿へと置き換え、最初の軽量な状態において貨物が欺瞞的な状態を保つように配慮しました。

川の中央の浅瀬に到達すると、悦に入ったロバは自らの予行演習通りの策略を熱心に実行し、水面下に自らの身を沈めました。しかしながら、溶解するどころか、海綿の毛細管微細構造は膨大な量の液体を猛烈に吸収しました。その獣が立ち上がろうと試みたとき、自らの重荷が指数関数的に増大して致命的な質量と化していることに、彼は完全な恐怖と共に気づきました。水分で飽和した積荷の下に閉じ込められたその喘ぐ動物は、疲弊した身体を向こうの土手へとどうにか這い上がらせる前に、危うく水没死するところでした。

**教訓**: 不誠実な近道に機械的に依存することは、基礎となる状況が根本的な変化を遂げたときに、必然的に壊滅的な誤算を生み出すことになる。

### 【出題頻出語彙・熟語ノート】
- **lucrative** [形] 利益の上がる、儲かる（*lucrative business*）
- **inadvertent** [形] 不注意な、故意でない（副詞 *inadvertently*）
- **equilibrium** [名] 平衡、均衡、心の平静（*lose one's equilibrium*）
- **extrapolate** [動] 〜を既知の事実から推定する、外挿する
- **fallacious** [形] 誤謬の、当てにならない（名詞 *fallacy*）
- **countermeasure** [名] 対抗策、防止策（*take countermeasures*）
- **substitute A with B** [熟] AをBで置き換える、代用する
- **colossal** [形] 途方もなく巨大な（*huge / immense*）
- **exponentially** [副] 指数関数的に、飛躍的に（形容詞 *exponential*）
- **undergo** [動] （変化・試練・手術など）を経験する、被る

### 【文法・構文ポイント】
1. **コロケーション表現 [undergo transformation]（変革・変容を遂げる）**
   - 準2級本文: *"...when underlying circumstances undergo a fundamental transformation."*
   - ポイント: `undergo`（〜を経験する）は `change, transformation, surgery, test` 等と結びつく最重要動詞。社会・自然の変化を説明する文章で頻出します。
2. **前置詞的対比表現 [rather than + 動名詞]（〜するのではなく）**
   - 準2級本文: *"However, rather than dissolving, the capillary microstructure..."*
   - ポイント: 「AではなくむしろB」を表す `rather than`。直後に動名詞 `dissolving` を伴い、予想された結果と正反対の事態を際立たせます。
